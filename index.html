Why this course exists

Most courses teach APIs. This course teaches how Angular thinks.

The goal is not to memorize new syntax. The goal is to understand the ideas behind Angular's evolution so you can confidently learn future features directly from the documentation.

Traditional learning

  • Use patterns without understanding trade-offs

  • Apply the concepts inside enterprise projects
