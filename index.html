Why this course exists

Stop memorizing. Start understanding.

The goal is to help you understand Angular deeply enough to learn future features directly from the documentation instead of waiting for someone else to explain every update.

Traditional learning

Copy. Paste. Forget.

  • Memorize APIs without understanding the problem.
  • Wait for tutorials whenever Angular changes.
  • Build small demos without architecture decisions.
  • Use patterns without understanding their trade-offs.
The CodeZetta approach

Understand. Build. Master.

  • Understand the original problem first.
  • Explore why Angular chose its solution.
  • Build important concepts from scratch.
  • Apply everything inside enterprise projects.
